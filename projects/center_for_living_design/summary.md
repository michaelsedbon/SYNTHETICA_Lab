# Center for Living Design (CLD)

**Status:** Active  
**Started:** 2026-03-23

---

## Overview

The Center for Living Design (CLD) is an interdisciplinary initiative bridging synthetic biology, bioart, and design. It aims to create a space where living systems are used as design materials — exploring the intersection of biological engineering, speculative design, and artistic practice.

The CLD operates as both a creative studio and a biology laboratory, supporting projects that range from bio-hybrid art installations to experimental evolution platforms. The center's long-term vision is to establish a physical space in France with a BSL1 laboratory, workshop, and exhibition/residency program.

## Core Activities

- **Biological experimentation** — Wet-lab work with microorganisms (algae, fungi, bacteria) for art and design projects
- **Hardware development** — Custom biosensors, bioreactors, and environmental control systems
- **Art & exhibition** — Bio-hybrid artworks and installations
- **Residencies & workshops** — Hosting artists and researchers for collaborative projects
- **Open science** — Publishing protocols, datasets, and designs openly

## Legal Structure

Planned as an **Association Loi 1901** (French non-profit association). See [Association & Lab Setup Research](knowledge/france_association_bsl1_research.md) for detailed requirements.

## Laboratory

Planned as a **BSL1** (Biosafety Level 1) laboratory. BSL1 is appropriate for work with well-characterized agents not known to cause disease in healthy adults. See [Association & Lab Setup Research](knowledge/france_association_bsl1_research.md) for regulatory requirements.
